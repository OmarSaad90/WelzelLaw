# Welzel Law — Product Context

## Product Purpose
Premium boutique law firm website for Welzel Law — a corporate and real estate law practice based in Ontario, Canada. The site serves as both a business development tool and a credibility signal for high-value clients. It must communicate expertise, trust, and approachability simultaneously.

## Register
brand

## Users
**Primary:** Ontario business owners, real estate investors, and entrepreneurs seeking legal counsel for corporate transactions, real estate closings, incorporations, and commercial agreements. Typically 35–60 years old, financially sophisticated, value discretion and professionalism.

**Secondary:** Referral partners — accountants, mortgage brokers, real estate agents — who may recommend Welzel Law to their clients.

## Brand Tone
Authoritative but not cold. Confident but not arrogant. Premium but not exclusionary. The voice is the trusted advisor in the room — someone who has seen every deal, stayed calm in every closing, and genuinely cares about the outcome.

Anti-patterns: corporate speak, aggressive sales language, buzzword soup ("trusted partner", "holistic solutions"), overly casual copy.

## Strategic Principles
1. **Boutique advantage** — small firm means direct partner access, not junior associate handoffs. Make this tangible.
2. **Ontario-specific expertise** — Toronto and Ontario real estate and corporate law is a niche. Own it.
3. **Trust through transparency** — clear practice areas, clear process, clear way to reach them.
4. **Premium positioning** — the site should feel expensive to visit. No clutter, no noise.

## Anti-References
- Generic legal template sites (Justia, LawDepot aesthetics)
- Purple-gradient SaaS aesthetics
- Stock photo gavels and scales of justice
- Navy blue + gold cliché legal branding
- Wall of text testimonials

## Practice Areas
- Corporate Law (incorporations, shareholders agreements, due diligence, M&A)
- Real Estate Law (residential and commercial closings, title insurance, mortgage)
- Business Law (contracts, commercial leases, employment agreements)
- Fractional General Counsel (embedded part-time legal executive for growing businesses, monthly fixed fee — the strategic and legal oversight of a full-time GC at $200K–$350K/year, without the salary commitment)

## Key Calls to Action
- Book a consultation
- Contact the firm
- View practice areas

## Location & Jurisdiction
Ontario, Canada. Primary market: Greater Toronto Area.
